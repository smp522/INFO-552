Welcome to the Philadelphia Trinket and Art Trading community!

Philadelphia trinket and art exchange boxes are run by the people for the people. These boxes are meant to spread joy and build kinship within their neighborhoods!

Each box is unique and has its own set of rules, but the general idea is to leave a trinket or piece of art in the box and take something that someone else has left. The boxes are a fun way to connect with your neighbors and share your creativity.

Give a trinket, take a trinket!
