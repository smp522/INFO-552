Professional Associations

Greater Philadelphia Law Library Association

Member since 2019

The Greater Philadelphia Law Library Association (“GPLLA”) is a regional chapter of the American Association of Law Libraries. Our goal is to foster cooperation, communication, and camaraderie amongst law librarians and other legal information professionals in the greater Philadelphia area, including those in law firms, academia, and in government. Towards that end, we sponsor educational and networking events, maintain a member directory, job board, and e-mail listserv, and offer scholarships and grants to students interested in law librarianship.

American Association of Law Libraries

Member since 2019

The American Association of Law Libraries advocates for access to legal information and support the legal information profession by cultivating the growth of our members, connecting them with each other, and championing the value of legal information and legal information professionals.

Association for Information Science and Technology (formerly Special Libraries Association)

Member since 2019

The Association for Information Science and Technology (ASIS&T) is the only professional association that bridges the gap between information science practice and research. For nearly 85 years, ASIS&T has been leading the search for new and better theories, techniques, and technologies to improve access to information.

Our members—thousands of researchers, developers, practitioners, students, and professors in the field of information science and technology from 50 countries around the world—have made ASIS&T an important part of their professional development.

Members share a common interest in improving the ways society stores, retrieves, analyzes, manages, archives and disseminates information.

International Legal Technology Association

Member since 2025

The International Legal Technology Association is the intersection of people, technology, and the business of law. When you have a challenge, ILTAns have a solution. ILTA members and business partners are subject matter experts in legal, business operations, and technological know-how.
