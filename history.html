History

Trinket and art trading boxes are part of the Worldwide Sidewalk Joy movement, which started in Portland, Oregon in 2022. Sidewalk Joy spots, like the original Dinorama pictured here, are free, curated public galleries, exchanges and displays. Installed in curb gardens, front yards or sides of buildings these projects were created to bring a bit of whimsy and inspiration to the community. Examples include Free Little Art Galleries, Puzzle Exchanges, Toy Swaps, year-round and often updated yard displays, Wishing Trees, and more!

The first Trinket Trading box in Philadelphia popped up in September 2025 at the corner of 15th and South Street. Since then, more boxes have been added all around the city.
